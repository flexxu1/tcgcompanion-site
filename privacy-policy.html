TCGCompanion Privacy Policy

TCGCompanion is a local-only Chrome extension. It has no account, no server, and no sync. Everything it records stays on your machine until you delete it or export it yourself.

Last verified against the extension source: 15 August 2026.

No network activity of its own

The shipped code contains no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket or sendBeacon call anywhere. There is no external server, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash reporting.

Prices are read from TCGplayer pages you already have open in your own browser session. The extension never fetches TCGplayer pages by itself.

What is stored locally

Everything below is kept in chrome.storage.local on your device.

For each card you save:

  • Card identity: name, set, condition, printing, language, product and SKU ids.
  • Prices: current market and low price, plus a bounded history of samples, each with a timestamp and the activity that produced it.
  • URLs: the TCGplayer product or listing address the card came from, and the card image address. Query strings and fragments are stripped from the saved-origin URL before storing.
  • Your own notes: free text you type on a card is kept as written.
  • Organisation: list membership, whether you marked it as a card you already have, an optional cart price cap, when it was saved, and where from — product page or listing page, which list you chose, and the price at that moment.

Also stored: your list names, whether each list is a deck or a collection, an optional per-list spend budget, and extension settings (junk-term filter, seller preferences, price polling and retention, and whether you accepted the cart risk notice).

Site access

Host access is limited to https://*.tcgplayer.com/*, and is used to:

  • Read visible page content to save card identity and visible pricing.
  • Filter visible listings by condition, and — only if you enable the junk listing filter — dim listings matching your junk terms.
  • Evaluate visible seller and listing text for local cart-matching decisions.
  • Capture price samples from TCGplayer tabs that are already open, and update local price history for saved cards on those pages.

That access also lets the extension see which TCGplayer tabs you currently have open — this is how “refresh from open tabs” finds pages to read. It is used only to locate TCGplayer tabs. No browsing outside tcgplayer.com is visible to it, and nothing about your tabs leaves your machine.

Permissions requested: storage and unlimitedStorage for local shortlist data, and alarms to schedule the optional open-tab price refresh, which is off by default.

Cart assistance

Cart tools are off by default and stay off until you enable them and accept an explicit risk notice.

When enabled, cart population uses your normal browser session and stops at add-to-cart. It does not automate checkout, payment or account changes, and does not spoof identity, solve CAPTCHAs, or bypass challenges. Automation is blocked outright on cart, checkout, order, payment, account, login and challenge pages.

Using it may still breach TCGplayer’s Terms of Service. That is your decision, which is why it is opt-in and gated behind the notice.

Backups and deletion

Export writes a JSON backup to a location you choose; import reads a file you choose and merges it without deleting existing records. An exported backup contains everything described above, including your notes and the stored URLs — treat the file the way you would treat the shortlist itself.

Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage. Individual cards and lists can be deleted from the dashboard at any time.

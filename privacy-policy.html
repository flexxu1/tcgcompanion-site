TCGCompanion Privacy Policy

TCGCompanion is a local-only Chrome extension. It has no account, no server, and no sync. Everything it records stays on your machine until you delete it or export it yourself.

Effective date: 3 September 2026 · Applies to TCGCompanion version 0.2.0 and later (first published 19 August 2026 for 0.1.0). Last verified against the extension source on 3 September 2026.

Network activity: one purpose-limited call

Deck import is the only feature that makes a network request of its own. When you run Import a deck, the card names you typed are sent to the Pokémon TCG API (api.pokemontcg.io) to look up card identities — names, sets, numbers and card images — so you can pick the exact printing you mean. Nothing else is ever sent: no prices, no browsing data, no identifiers, and nothing at all unless you use that feature. Prices are never taken from this API.

Cards added through deck import store three extra identity fields: where the match came from (importedFrom, always the Pokémon TCG API), the API's card id (importApiId), and the API's product pointer (importUrl). These are provenance only — imported cards carry no price until you open their TCGplayer page yourself, exactly like any other unpriced card.

Beyond that one call there is no external server, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or crash reporting, and no other fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket or sendBeacon call anywhere in the shipped code. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships — see Changes to this policy below.

Prices are read from TCGplayer pages you already have open in your own browser session. The extension never fetches TCGplayer pages by itself.

Card pictures. When the dashboard shows a card's picture, your browser loads that image from the address stored with the card — TCGplayer's own image servers for cards saved from the site, or the Pokémon TCG API's image host for imported cards — the same request the site itself makes when you browse. Nothing but the image address is sent. Only those two hosts are ever loaded from; a backup naming any other host renders “No image” instead.

What the developer collects: nothing

The developer does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, rent, or share any user data. There is no category of personal information gathered by the developer, and there are no third parties with whom information is shared, because no information ever leaves your device.

Everything described in the next section is stored by the extension on your own computer, where only you can reach it. It is disclosed here because an extension must explain how it handles data even when that data never leaves the device.

What is stored locally

Everything below is kept in chrome.storage.local on your device.

For each card you save:

  • Card identity: name, set, condition, printing, language, product and SKU ids.
  • Prices: current market and low price, plus a bounded history of samples, each with a timestamp and the activity that produced it.
  • URLs: the TCGplayer product or listing address the card came from, and the card image address. Query strings and fragments are stripped from the saved-origin URL before storing.
  • Your own notes: free text you type on a card is kept as written.
  • Organisation: list membership, whether you marked it as a card you already have, how many copies you own, an optional cart price cap, when it was saved, and where from — product page or listing page, which list you chose, and the price at that moment.

Also stored: your list names, whether each list is a deck or a collection, an optional per-list spend budget, and extension settings (junk-term filter, seller preferences, price polling and retention, and whether you accepted the cart risk notice).

Housekeeping records. Alongside the above the extension keeps a small amount of internal bookkeeping, listed here because this page claims to enumerate everything it stores: the storage key it filed each card under and which identifier that key came from, a version marker recording which release read the page, a cursor noting how far the optional price refresh has walked through your saved cards, and the date of your last backup export so it can remind you when one is overdue. None of it describes you; it exists so the extension can find its own records and avoid repeating work.

The extension does not ask for and does not store your name, email address, postal address, phone number, payment details, or TCGplayer account credentials.

Site access

Host access is limited to https://*.tcgplayer.com/* and, for deck import only, https://api.pokemontcg.io/*. The TCGplayer access is used to:

  • Read visible page content to save card identity and visible pricing.
  • Filter visible listings by condition, and — only if you enable the junk listing filter — dim listings matching your junk terms.
  • Evaluate visible seller and listing text for local cart-matching decisions.
  • Capture price samples from TCGplayer tabs that are already open, and update local price history for saved cards on those pages.

That access also lets the extension see which TCGplayer tabs you currently have open — this is how “refresh from open tabs” finds pages to read. It is used only to locate TCGplayer tabs. No browsing outside tcgplayer.com is visible to it, and nothing about your tabs leaves your machine.

Permissions requested: storage and unlimitedStorage for local shortlist data, and alarms to schedule the optional open-tab price refresh, which is off by default.

Limited Use

TCGCompanion's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose — a local TCGplayer card watchlist with listing filters, price snapshots and manual cart-prep tools.

It is never transferred to third parties, never used or transferred for advertising of any kind, including personalised, re-targeted or interest-based advertising, and never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. No human reads your data, because no data reaches the developer.

Cart assistance

Cart tools are off by default and stay off until you enable them and accept an explicit risk notice.

When enabled, cart population uses your normal browser session and stops at add-to-cart. It does not automate checkout, payment or account changes, and does not spoof identity, solve CAPTCHAs, or bypass challenges. Automation is blocked outright on cart, checkout, order, payment, account, login and challenge pages.

Using it may still breach TCGplayer’s Terms of Service. That is your decision, which is why it is opt-in and gated behind the notice.

Your control, retention and deletion

Because the data is on your machine, you hold it directly. You can view and edit every saved card and list in the dashboard, remove cards individually or in bulk, and delete whole lists. To remove everything at once, uninstall the extension — Chrome deletes its local storage with it. There is no in-product “erase all data” button.

Retention is yours to set: price-history samples are bounded and pruned according to the retention setting on the options page. Nothing is retained by the developer, because nothing is sent to the developer. There is no account to close and no deletion request to submit — and equally, no way for the developer to recover data you delete.

Export writes a JSON backup to a location you choose; import reads a file you choose and merges it without deleting existing records. An exported backup contains everything described above, including your notes and the stored URLs — treat the file the way you would treat the collection itself.

The cart risk acceptance is deliberately not restored from a backup. It is consent given on this install, so a restored profile asks you to read and accept the notice again before cart tools or scheduled refreshes can run.

Security

Your data is protected by Chrome's own extension storage isolation and by the security of your computer and browser profile. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server to breach, no database of users, and no data in transit to intercept.

Two honest limits: anyone with access to your unlocked computer and browser profile can read the extension's data, and an exported backup file is a plain JSON file with no encryption. Store backups accordingly.

Do Not Track

TCGCompanion does not track you across websites or over time, and does not build any profile of you. Because it performs no tracking at all, there is nothing for a browser “Do Not Track” signal to switch off, and the extension does not alter its behaviour in response to one.

No other party conducts tracking through this extension. This disclosure is provided under California Business and Professions Code section 22575(b)(5).

Children

TCGCompanion is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — and in fact collects no personal information from anyone, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page is updated and the revised policy is posted at this same address before the change takes effect in the extension. Material changes to what the extension does with your data will also be disclosed in the extension's own interface and in the Chrome Web Store listing, so that a change in data handling is never applied silently to an existing install.
